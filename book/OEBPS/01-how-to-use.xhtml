How to use this book

This is a sample. The trip is invented: three days, three places. The method is the real one.

There is one chapter for each day. Read the evening before, or on the train in the morning. Every chapter goes in the same order: first the place, what it is and what happened there; then how people live with it; then one fact to tell at the table; then what to look at; and at the end, where each fact came from.

Every Japanese word appears three ways: the way it is written, how it sounds in our alphabet, and what it means. Like this: 旅 · tabi · a journey. Say it out loud. By the third day you will recognise a few of them on signs.

There are no times, prices or bookings anywhere in this book. Those change, and they belong in your tickets and your calendar. What is in here does not change from one week to the next.
